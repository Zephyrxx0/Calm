# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Everyday civilians aiming to track their carbon footprint and maintain/check their daily environmental goals.

## Product Purpose
To provide a serene, low-stress environment where users can reflect on their carbon footprint, interact with an AI coach (the "interview" aspect), and manage personal sustainability goals without feeling overwhelmed. Success means users feel mindful and grounded during their daily check-ins.

## Brand Personality
Peaceful, zen, relaxing. Grounded, organic, and approachable.

## Brand References
- zen-browser website (clean, focused, distraction-free)
- claude.ai (soft, minimal UI with confident typography)
- anything.com

## Anti-references
- Chaotic, loud, or overstimulating corporate tech dashboards
- Hyper-gamified or aggressive "tracker" apps that induce guilt or anxiety
- Sterile "SaaS" aesthetics lacking human or natural touch

## Design Principles
1. **Invite reflection, not reaction**: Provide breathing room and pacing so users can process their footprint calmly.
2. **Organic over mechanical**: Favor nature-inspired doodles, earthy tones, and soft transitions over rigid grids and mechanical UI.
3. **Calm the workflow**: Tracking carbon shouldn't feel like a chore; it should feel like tending a garden. Focus on peaceful progress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web best practices (WCAG 2.1 AA). Ensure color contrast is maintained despite soft, earthy palettes. Provide reduced-motion equivalents for any nature-inspired animations.
