# Project: Calm

Calm is a carbon awareness platform that asks you to do something no app asks anymore — slow down. The aesthetic is intentional: soft, quiet, and unhurried. A space where environmental awareness feels like a breath, not a burden.

The name works on two levels. Calm as in stillness — take a breath before you act. Calm as in the quiet that comes from clarity — once you know your numbers, anxiety becomes action.

## Core Features

Three features, designed with quiet intention:

1.  **The Interview (AI Carbon Coach)** — The AI converses with you gently, one question at a time. It asks about your commute, your diet, your energy bill. At the end, it presents your carbon profile as a clear, beautiful summary.
2.  **The Ledger (Receipt & Bill Scanner)** — Upload your grocery receipts, electricity bills, fuel slips. Claude reads them line by line and enters each item into your carbon ledger — clean, organized, easy to scan.
3.  **The Summary (Output)** — Your personal carbon report: a clear, well-structured overview with your total footprint, a breakdown by category, key insights from your answers, and your top 3 actions — presented with calm clarity.
