# Project: Calm

Calm is a carbon awareness platform that asks you to do something no app asks anymore — slow down and actually read. The aesthetic is intentional: by presenting your environmental impact in the language of a vintage broadsheet, it signals that this information is worth pausing for. The medium is the message.

The name works on two levels. Calm as in stillness — take a breath before you act. Calm as in the quiet that comes from clarity — once you know your numbers, anxiety becomes action.

## Core Features

Three features, three editorial metaphors:

1.  **The Interview (AI Carbon Coach)** — The AI converses with you like a journalist doing a profile piece. It asks about your commute, your diet, your energy bill. Unhurried, one question at a time. At the end, it typesets your carbon profile as a personalized front-page edition.
2.  **The Ledger (Receipt & Bill Scanner)** — Upload your grocery receipts, electricity bills, fuel slips. Claude reads them line by line and enters each item into your carbon ledger — styled like a financial column or classifieds section, not a dashboard.
3.  **The Edition (Output)** — Your personal newspaper: a typeset, column-formatted carbon report with a bold headline ("YOUR FOOTPRINT: 230 KG ABOVE NATIONAL AVERAGE"), pull quotes, a breakdown by category in column layout, and a ranked list of your top 3 actions — printed like editorial recommendations.
