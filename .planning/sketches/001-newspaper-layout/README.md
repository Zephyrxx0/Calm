# Sketch 001: Newspaper Masthead & Layout

## Design Question
Which newspaper layout style — clean 2000s broadsheet, dense 1990s tabloid, or modern 2008 hybrid — best communicates the carbon data while feeling authentically like a printed newspaper?

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/001-newspaper-layout/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Clean Broadsheet (2000s)** — Cream paper (#FAFAF8), 4 columns, Playfair Display masthead, thin rules, subtle fold crease, spacious layout. Inspired by Burlington Free Press circa 2000.
- **B: Dense Tabloid (1990s)** — Yellowed paper (#F5F0E8), 5 columns, double-rule masthead, tighter leading, smaller type, all-caps headlines. Higher contrast, cheaper newsprint feel.
- **C: Modern Hybrid (2008)** — White paper, 4 columns, sans-serif kickers mixing with serif headlines, sidebar box, cleaner layout. Campus newspaper style circa 2008.

## What to Look For
- **Readability:** Can you scan the stories and find what you want quickly?
- **Authenticity:** Which variant feels most like a real printed newspaper?
- **Data density:** Does the layout accommodate enough content without feeling cramped?
- **Masthead presence:** Does "The Daily Calm" carry proper weight at the top?
- **Fold line:** Does the subtle fold crease in Variant A enhance the vintage feel?
