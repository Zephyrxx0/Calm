# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Realistic vintage newspaper front page for Calm's carbon summary. 2000-era broadsheet aesthetic: cream paper, 4-column layout, Playfair Display masthead, PT Serif body, SVG filter effects (grain, halftone, fold crease, ink bleed, edge wear). Dynamic content from interview and daily tracking data. 17×11 landscape export.

## Reference Points
- Burlington Free Press (2000 broadsheet)
- The Winonan (2000s campus paper)
- 1980s East Village independent newspaper
