Your Carbon Year in Review

8.4 tonnes CO₂e / year

By The Calm Newsroom

How You Stack Up

Your annual carbon output of 8.4 tonnes sits modestly above the national average of 7.2 tonnes, reflecting a commuting-heavy lifestyle balanced by plant-forward eating. Transportation accounts for 42% of your total — a pattern the Calm research team sees increasingly among urban professionals who drive solo but eat sustainably. The largest single contributor remains your daily 45-minute commute, burning roughly 2.8 tonnes of CO₂e per year.

Combined with one round-trip flight to visit family, aviation and ground transport dominate the ledger. Your home energy use, at 18% of the total, is actually below average for your housing type — suggesting your insulation and appliance choices are already working in your favor.

See CARBON, Page A3

By Emma Hartley, Staff Writer

Your Top Three Actions for the Year Ahead

1. Swap one commute day. Working from home just one extra day per week would cut 0.7 tonnes — nearly 8% of your total — without any lifestyle disruption. Your employer already supports hybrid work, so this is purely a habit change.

2. Turn down the thermostat. Home energy is your quiet contributor at 18%. Dropping two degrees in winter months could save 0.4 tonnes annually, and you would barely notice the difference in a well-insulated space.

3. Eat the seasons. Your diet is already 60% plant-based. Going to 80% — swapping beef for lentils in two weekly meals — would trim another 0.3 tonnes. Small change, meaningful compounding.
